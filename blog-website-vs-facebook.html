Website vs Facebook Page: Which Is Better for a Business?

By Mohammad Abdullah

In today's digital world, having an online presence is important for almost every business. But many business owners ask the same question: should they create a professional website or simply use a Facebook Page?

Both platforms can help a business connect with customers, but they serve different purposes. Understanding the difference can help businesses build a stronger online presence.

What Does a Facebook Page Offer?

A Facebook Page gives businesses a simple way to communicate with their audience. Businesses can share updates, photos, offers, customer reviews and important announcements.

Facebook can also be useful for social media marketing, because potential customers can discover a business while using the platform.

What Does a Website Offer?

A website gives a business its own dedicated space on the internet. It allows the business to present its services, information, portfolio, contact details and other important content in one professional location.

1. A More Professional Presence

A professionally designed website can give customers a clear understanding of what a business does and how they can get in touch.

Unlike a social media profile, a website can be designed around the business's own branding, structure and goals.

2. More Control Over Your Brand

A Facebook Page operates within Facebook's design and platform. A website gives a business much greater control over its layout, colours, typography, content and customer experience.

3. All Business Information in One Place

A website can bring together services, about information, testimonials, project examples, frequently asked questions and contact options.

Instead of asking customers to search through social media posts, businesses can organize their information into clear pages and sections.

Website vs Facebook Page

Feature Website Facebook Page
Design control High Limited
Brand customization Extensive Limited
Business information Fully customizable Platform-based
Customer communication Forms, email & more Messenger & comments
Social engagement Limited Strong

Website and Facebook can work together.

Businesses do not necessarily have to choose only one. Social media can help attract and engage customers, while a website can provide a central destination for detailed business information.

Why Businesses Can Benefit From Both

Social media can be useful for building conversations, sharing updates and reaching an audience. A website can provide a more structured and professional destination for people who want to learn more about the business.

For example, a business can promote a service on Facebook and direct interested customers to its website where they can explore the service, view previous work and send an enquiry.

What Should a Business Choose?

The right setup depends on the business, its audience, marketing strategy and goals.

A Facebook Page can be useful for social interaction and audience engagement, while a website can provide a dedicated and customizable home for the business online.
