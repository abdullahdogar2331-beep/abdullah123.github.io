Website vs Facebook Page: Which Is Better for a Business?

Published September 16, 2026

Facebook is a useful platform for businesses, but having your own website gives your business a dedicated online presence. So, which one should a business choose?

What Is a Facebook Business Page?

A Facebook Page allows a business to share posts, photos, updates, offers and contact information with its audience. It can be useful for connecting with existing and potential customers.

What Is a Business Website?

A business website is an online home for your business. You can use it to explain your services, display your work, provide contact information and give customers important information about your company.

1. A Website Gives You More Control

On a website, you control the structure and presentation of your business information. You can create pages for different services, projects, FAQs and contact options.

2. A Website Can Build Professional Credibility

A well-designed website can give customers a convenient place to learn about a business before contacting it. Professional design, clear information and an organized layout can create a stronger online presence.

3. Social Media Is Still Valuable

This does not mean businesses should stop using Facebook. Social media can help businesses communicate with their audience and share updates.

4. The Best Approach Can Be Using Both

For many businesses, a website and social media can work together. Social media can help attract attention, while the website can provide detailed information about the business and its services.

Conclusion

A Facebook Page and a business website serve different purposes. Social media can help businesses connect with their audience, while a website can provide a dedicated and professional online presence.
